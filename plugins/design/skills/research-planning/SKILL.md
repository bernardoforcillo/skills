# Research planning

No decision, no study. Start from the decision the research informs.

## Method

1. Write the **decision**, who makes it, and when.
2. List the **research questions**.
3. Choose the method by the three axes plus stage; fill the matrix in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design/templates/research-plan.md`.
4. Set the sample from NN/g's guide: **5** per qualitative round, **≥20** quantitative, **≥15 per group** for card sorting, **39** for stable eyetracking heatmaps. Five is a per-round budget — plan several rounds with redesign between; never call it a prevalence statistic.
5. Interviews: ask about **recent critical incidents**, not predictions.
6. Synthesis: affinity diagram — silent individual writing, group before naming, guard against dominant voices.
7. State the **evidence level** (L1–L5) the study will reach and what it will *not* license.

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design/references/ux-process.md`.

## Delegate

The `design-lead` agent runs an isolated planning pass. For why-it-works-on-a-person questions, the `skills` plugin's `neuro-design` desk is the evidence base (optional peer).
